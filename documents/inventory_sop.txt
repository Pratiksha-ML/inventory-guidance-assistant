# Inventory Management Standard Operating Procedure

## 1. Purpose
This document defines basic inventory management guidelines.

## 2. Minimum Stock Policy
Every product must maintain a minimum stock level as defined in the inventory database.

## 3. Low Stock Identification
A product is considered low stock when its available quantity is below its defined minimum stock level.

## 4. Replenishment Guidelines
When a product falls below its minimum stock level, the inventory team should review its replenishment requirements.

The suggested replenishment quantity should bring the available stock back to at least the minimum stock level.

## 5. Out of Stock Policy
When available stock reaches zero, the inventory team should prioritize reviewing the product availability and replenishment requirements.

## 6. Approval
All purchase orders and stock-changing actions require approval from an authorized team member.
